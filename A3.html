Assignment 3: Video Game Sales (Vega-Lite)

Eight visualizations exploring global sales patterns across genre, platform, time, and region.

Visualization 1A: Global Sales by Genre

Which genres generate the highest total global sales across all platforms?

Action and Sports have the highest total global sales across all platforms. After the top three genres, sales drop clearly, showing that a few major genres dominate the market.

Visualization 1B: Global Sales by Platform and Genre

Which platforms generate the highest total global sales, and does their dominance differ across genres? (Top 5 platforms only)

PS2 has the highest total sales among the top five platforms, but each platform shows a different genre pattern. Wii has stronger sales in Misc and Platform games, while PS3 and X360 depend more on Action and Shooter titles.

Visualization 2A: Sales Over Time by Platform

How have total global sales changed over time for the top 5 platforms?

Sales for each top platform follow a clear life cycle pattern, with strong growth, a peak period, and then decline. PS2 dominates early in the 2000s, while Wii peaks sharply around 2008, and X360 and PS3 reach their highest sales slightly later before gradually decreasing.

Visualization 2B: Sales Over Time by Genre

How have total global sales changed over time for the top 5 genres?

Action and Sports consistently have the highest yearly sales, with clear peaks around the late 2000s. After 2010, most genres show a steady decline, suggesting a shift in overall market momentum.

Visualization 3A: Regional Sales by Platform

For the top 5 platforms by global sales, how do regional sales compare across North America, Europe, Japan, and Other regions?

North America is the strongest region by a wide margin. Even the lowest-selling platform in North America is still higher than the highest-selling platform in Europe, which shows how much the top 5 platforms depend on NA sales compared to the other regions.

Visualization 3B: Japan vs North America

Which platforms have the highest sales in Japan compared to North America (top 10 platforms in each region)?

Japan and North America show very different platform preferences. North America is led by X360 and PS2, while Japan favors Nintendo and older PlayStation platforms such as DS, SNES, and PS.

Visualization 4A: Blockbusters and the Long Tail

Are video game sales concentrated among a small number of blockbuster titles, or distributed more evenly across many games?

Most games sell under 1 million units, while only a very small number reach high sales levels. This indicates a long-tail pattern where a few blockbuster titles drive a large share of total sales.

Visualization 4B: Publisher Diversity and Sales

Do publishers differ in how diversified they are across genres, and does diversification relate to higher total sales?

Publishers that release games across more genres generally earn higher total global sales, which suggests that spreading risk across different types of games can help overall performance. It also shows that long-term growth is often about building a broad portfolio, not just one hit game.
