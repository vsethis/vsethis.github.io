D3 Interaction

This page shows an interactive D3 canvas. Clicking anywhere inside the canvas adds a new circle at the exact clicked position. A maximum of 10 circles can appear at one time.

Click to Add Circles

Each click adds one new circle. When more than 10 circles are added, the oldest circle is removed.
